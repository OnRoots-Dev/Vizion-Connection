# Product Vision

## Why
Sports effort is scattered across social media, calendars, portfolios, event pages, and private relationships. Vizion Connection aims to make the sports economy and the people inside it visible through activity, place, identity, and support.

## Difference from existing SNS
Generic SNS optimizes content consumption. Vizion Connection should optimize discovery of sports activity and people. A post is an expression; an Activity is an action with context; a Moment captures meaningful evidence from that action.

## Portfolio vs Activity
Portfolio answers “who is this person after I find them?” Activity answers “what is happening in sports right now?” Portfolio therefore supports conversion after discovery, while Activity drives recurring discovery.

## Crew
Crew represents the audience, supporters, participants, spectators, and community members around sport. Crew should be able to discover activity and contribute support without pretending every user is an athlete or professional.

## Business
Business should discover Athletes, Trainers, Crew, Communities, Activities, Moments, and Places, then develop support, PR, sponsorship, or collaboration opportunities. ## Why Map
A spatial interface answers questions a feed cannot: what is happening near me, where are active people, and what sports exist around this place? Map is therefore a discovery surface, not decoration.

## Why Activity is central
Activity creates a repeatable unit that can be scheduled, placed, discovered, reacted to, and later transformed into a Moment. It creates a bridge between the real world and the digital profile.

## Strategic loop
Map creates context → Activity creates action → Moment creates evidence → Cheer/Comment/Connection creates social reinforcement → Discovery creates new relationships → Activity creates the next loop.
