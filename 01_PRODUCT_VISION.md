# Product Vision

Status: **Planned direction; current implementation is a profile/dashboard platform.**

Vizion exists because sporting effort is often invisible between a profile page and a major result. Existing social networks optimize broad attention; sportsfolio services optimize credentials. Vizion makes the living activity around sport discoverable, then lets people inspect a credible identity and portfolio.

Portfolio is a detail surface after a person has been found. Activity is the center because it gives athletes a lightweight habit, gives crew a reason to return, gives trainers a way to surface sessions, and gives businesses meaningful context.

Crew participates through attending, cheering, commenting, joining activities, and community—not only watching. Businesses participate as visible sports actors that can discover people, places, activities, and communities, then express support, PR, sponsorship interest, or collaboration. Vizion does not broker monetary agreements at MVP.

The map is a spatial index: nearby activity, moments, people, and sport become explorable without turning the feed into noise. The design must be recognizably Vizion rather than a copy of any social app.
