# UI Reference

References such as Instagram, TikTok, X, BeReal, Strava, Nike, Apple, and modern map products are used only to study interaction patterns—not copied visual language, layouts, assets, or brand cues.

Vizion interaction principles: a Cheer gives a brief gold reward response; Connection communicates reciprocal human trust; Moments invite a focused response; Map cards reveal progressively; cards lift subtly and preserve context during transitions. Use the existing motion recipes and reduced-motion alternative. Maps must cluster at low zoom, summarize before expanding, and make location privacy legible.
