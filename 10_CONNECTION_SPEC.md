# Connection Specification

**Cheer** is the Current encouragement reaction (like-like mechanics, different social meaning). It must remain distinct from generic likes.

**Connection** is Planned MVP and is not a Follow clone: it represents a person-to-person relationship with explicit state and consent. Minimum lifecycle: requested → accepted or declined/cancelled; either party can remove. Public discovery does not imply connection. Existing `user_follows` and bond-related routes are Current/legacy-adjacent mechanisms; do not rename or repurpose them without a migration and compatibility plan.

Comments are Current for News and Planned for Moments. Require authenticated authorship, target visibility checks, moderation capability, rate limiting, and owner/admin removal rules.
