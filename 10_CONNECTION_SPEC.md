# Connection Specification

## Meaning
Connection means a meaningful relationship between people in the sports ecosystem. It is not a renamed Follow.

## Distinction
- Cheer: support/action.
- Follow: current social subscription concept may exist in code, but it is not the product definition of Connection.
- Connection: relationship formed through an intentional interaction and potentially visible in network context.

## MVP
Define create/request/accept or equivalent state transitions only after UX and permission rules are approved. The exact protocol is currently **Undecided**; do not infer it from existing follows.

## UX
Connection formation should feel consequential but lightweight. Avoid spammy growth mechanics. Show relationship state clearly.

## Security
Relationship mutation must be authenticated, ownership-aware, rate-limited where appropriate, validated, and protected from duplicate requests.
