# Business Specification

Status: Business checkout, ad slots, ads, offers, sponsorship records, and hubs are **Current** in limited form. Ecosystem discovery is **Planned**.

Businesses are participants, not merely ad buyers. They may create identity, activity, and moments and discover public athletes, trainers, crew, communities, activities, moments, and places. Outcomes can be Support, PR, Sponsor interest, and Collaboration. At MVP, Vizion presents context and intent only; it does not negotiate, guarantee, or intermediate a monetary contract.

Only public/authorized data may be used for discovery. Business role does not bypass user visibility, consent, RLS, or anti-spam controls.
