# Open Lab Skill

## Purpose
Provide a controlled place for experiments, emerging sports ideas, community initiatives, or future product capabilities without polluting core objects.

## Rules
- Keep experimental data separate from production-critical flows unless approved.
- Label experiments clearly.
- Do not use Open Lab to bypass permissions or moderation.
- Reuse Activity/Profile/Place concepts where the experiment naturally fits.

## Status
MVP foundation planned; exact user-facing workflow is Undecided.
