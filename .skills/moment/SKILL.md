# Moment Skill

## Purpose
Moment is the evidence/highlight layer that makes an Activity emotionally and visually discoverable.

## Rules
- Prefer linking Moment to an Activity where possible.
- Media should be first-class but never mandatory unless the approved UX says so.
- Preserve author, timestamp, place/context, visibility, and source Activity.
- Keep creation lightweight.
- Do not treat Moment as a second generic feed system.

## Status
Planned unless a dedicated production Moment model/API is verified.

## Safety
Respect visibility and ownership; validate media references; do not expose private content through map/discovery surfaces.
